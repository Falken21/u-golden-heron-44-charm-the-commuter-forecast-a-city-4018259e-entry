Entry for the make.fun bounty [Charm the Commuter: Forecast a City That Isn't](https://make.fun/bounty/u-golden-heron-44-charm-the-commuter-forecast-a-city-4018259e): one line of weather, at most 140 characters, for a city that does not exist.

## The entry

> Snoozeburg: fog until 8, then sunshine with a light breeze. The 8:04 is running twenty minutes late — but so are you, so it evens out.

Snoozeburg is a city that runs on snooze-button time: the morning fog lifts at 8, the sun comes out for the commute, and the 8:04 train is twenty minutes late — which, for once, suits its passengers perfectly.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
